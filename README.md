### Tracking a Phone Number
1. Click **"Track Number"** on the home screen.
2. Type a phone number (include country code, like +1 for USA).
3. Press **"Search"**.
4. Wait 5-10 seconds – the app shows the location on a map.

### Translating Text
1. Click **"Translate"**.
2. Choose the language you want to translate from and to.
3. Type your text OR upload a document (PDF or Word).
4. Press **"Go"** – your translation appears instantly.

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting

### Common Issues and Fixes

**"The app won't open"**
- Make sure your Windows is updated
- Right-click the app and choose "Run as administrator"
- Restart your computer and try again

**"Can't find my phone's location"**
- Check that you entered the correct country code
- Make sure you have internet connection
- Some numbers may be unlisted – try again later

**"Translation is slow"**
- This usually means your internet is slow
- Try closing other programs that use lots of data
- Reduce the size of documents you upload

**"Map is blank"**
- Allow the app to access your location settings
- Check your internet connection
- Refresh the map by pressing F5
